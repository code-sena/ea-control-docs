# Product Vision

> The vision is the team's north star. All sprints, design decisions,
> and trade-offs are evaluated against this vision.
>
> It must be ambitious yet achievable, inspiring but specific.

---

## Vision statement

> Use the Geoffrey Moore template:

**For** users of educational environments

**who** need to monitor and understand environmental conditions and their changes over time

**the** EduAirControl

**is a** web-based environmental monitoring and data analysis platform

**that** centralizes information about temperature, humidity, CO₂, and noise levels and provides historical analysis by day, week, month, and year

**unlike** fragmented or non-centralized ways of consulting environmental information

**our product** provides a centralized dashboard for monitoring and analyzing environmental conditions in educational environments.

---

## Team mission

> Our team exists to develop a platform that centralizes environmental information from educational environments, making temperature, humidity, CO₂, and noise data accessible and understandable through monitoring and historical analysis.

---

## Strategic pillars

Pillars are the focus areas that take us from mission to vision.

They should be few (3-5) and consistent over time.

| Pillar | Description | Success metrics |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Environmental Monitoring | Provide access to environmental information from educational environments, including temperature, humidity, CO₂, and noise level. | Availability of environmental data |
| Data Analysis | Allow users to understand environmental conditions through historical analysis by day, week, month, and year. | Availability of historical analysis by defined periods |
| User Experience | Provide a clear and accessible dashboard for consulting environmental information. | Dashboard usage and user feedback |
| Reliability | Maintain consistent access to environmental information and analysis functionality. | System availability and successful data processing |

## Product principles

These principles guide design and prioritization decisions when there are trade-offs.

**1.Data-driven decisions:** Environmental information should be presented in a way that helps users understand the conditions of educational environments.
**2.Clarity first:** Environmental variables and historical analysis should be presented clearly and accessibly through the dashboard.
**3.Focus on the defined scope:** New functionality should support the monitoring and analysis of educational environmental conditions without introducing features outside the current product scope.
**4.Progressive evolution:** The product should evolve according to validated user needs and project learning rather than assuming future requirements.
